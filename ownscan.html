OwnScan

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android app "OwnScan" (the "App"), published by Wonastral, handles information when you use it. OwnScan turns paper documents into PDF files. It was designed so that your documents stay yours: they are processed and stored on your phone, not on our servers.

2. In Short

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address requested.
  • Your scans, the recognized text and your signature are stored only on your device.
  • Wonastral operates no server for this App and receives none of your documents.
  • The free version shows an ad banner from Google AdMob, with your consent where the law requires it. The ad-free version loads no advertising component at all.
  • You can delete all your data at any time from the App's settings.

3. Your Documents Stay on Your Device

The pages you scan or import, the PDF files produced, the text recognized in them, your saved signature and your settings are stored in the App's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read them, and they are never sent to Wonastral.

They are kept until you delete them. You can delete a page, a document, or everything at once (Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data). Uninstalling the App also deletes them.

4. Camera and Text Recognition

Pages are captured with the document scanner provided by Google Play services; the App itself does not request the camera permission. Edge detection, filters, PDF creation and text recognition (Google ML Kit, with a model embedded in the App) all run on your device, without an internet connection. The content of your images and texts is never sent online.

5. Technical Data From Google Components

Like any app using them, the Google components embedded in OwnScan (ML Kit and Google Play services) may send Google limited technical information to measure their reliability: device model, Android version, app version, performance metrics, error codes and an identifier specific to this installation. This information never includes the content of your documents. It is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties.

Details: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and Google's privacy policy, policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version displays a banner at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. There are no full-screen ads. AdMob is an independent third party that may process certain information to serve ads, including personalized ads where you have agreed to it.

Depending on your consent and device settings, AdMob may process:

  • your Android advertising ID (a resettable identifier);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, Android version, language);
  • interactions with ads (impressions, clicks).

This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. AdMob-specific details: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before the advertising component starts. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Manage ad consent. If you refuse, the App works exactly the same; only non-personalized ads may be shown.

If you buy the ad-free version, the advertising component is never started: no ad request is made and the advertising ID is not read.

7. In-App Purchase

The ad-free version is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Wonastral never sees your payment details; the App only receives from Google Play the confirmation that the purchase was made, to remove the ads.

8. Sharing and Exporting

A document leaves the App only when you decide so: when you share it (Android share sheet), save it to a folder you choose, to Downloads or to the gallery. The app or service you choose then handles it under its own privacy policy.

9. Android Backup

If Android backup is turned on on your phone, your documents are included in your Google account backup so you can find them on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google, is encrypted by Android, and Wonastral has no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings (System → Backup).

10. Permissions

OwnScan asks for no access to your location, contacts, microphone or photo library. The permissions it declares are:

  • Internet and network state: for the ad banner and the Google Play purchase only;
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob in the free version only;
  • Google Play Billing: for the ad-free purchase;
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier only): to save a PDF to Downloads when you ask for it.

11. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw your consent at any time. Personalized advertising relies on your consent; the App's other features rely on our legitimate interest in providing the service you requested.

As Wonastral holds no data about you, your documents are under your sole control on your device: you can export or delete them yourself at any time. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use the consent form in the App, your Android advertising settings, or contact Google directly.

For any question, write to us (see Contact). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in France, the CNIL.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age applicable in your country) and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

13. Security

Your documents are stored in the App's private storage, protected by Android. Wonastral keeps no database of users that could be breached. Network exchanges made by Google components are encrypted (HTTPS).

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the App or in the law. The new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
