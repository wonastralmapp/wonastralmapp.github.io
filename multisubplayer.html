MultiSubPlayer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application "MultiSubPlayer" (the "App"), published by Wonastral, handles information when you use it on an Android device. MultiSubPlayer is a media player and language-learning tool that lets you watch or listen to your own video and audio files with subtitles, generate and translate subtitles, use text-to-speech, look up words, and create AnkiDroid flashcards. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

2. No Account and No Data Collected by the Developer

The App does not require an account and does not collect, store, or share any personal data about you on our side. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party crash-reporting tools.

Wonastral does not operate any server for this App. We never receive your media files, subtitles, settings, or usage data.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Everything the App creates or remembers is stored locally on your device, in the App's private storage. This includes:

  • App settings and preferences (language, playback and display options);
  • API keys you enter (for example your Groq API key);
  • Favorites, bookmarks, and saved words;
  • Generated subtitles, translations, and text-to-speech audio;
  • Your subtitle edits and synchronization adjustments;
  • Speech-to-text and text-to-speech models you choose to download.

This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and can be erased at any time from the App's settings ("Reset app data") or by uninstalling the App.

4. Media and Subtitle Files

The App plays video and audio files that you already have on your device and reads the associated subtitle files. To do this, the App requests access to media and files on your device. Broad file access ("All files access") is used only to automatically detect subtitle files that match the name of the media you open. Your files are read and processed locally and are not uploaded to any Wonastral server.

The App does not use the microphone. Speech-to-text works on the audio contained in the media file you open, not on live recording.

5. Optional Online Feature — Groq API (Subtitle Generation & Translation)

The App offers optional features that use Groq, an AI service operated by Groq, Inc., to generate subtitles from a media file's audio (speech-to-text) and to produce high-quality translations. These features are optional and work on a "bring your own key" basis: they run only if you obtain your own free Groq API key and enter it in the App's settings.

When you use these features, the relevant data is sent directly from your device to Groq's servers using your own key: short audio segments extracted from the media you selected (for subtitle generation) and subtitle text (for translation). This data is processed by Groq to return the transcription or translation and is subject to Groq's own privacy policy.

If you never enter a Groq API key, no audio or text is ever sent to Groq. You can obtain a key at console.groq.com/keys and review Groq's practices at groq.com/privacy-policy.

6. Optional Online Feature — Google Translate

As an alternative translation option, the App can send subtitle or selected text to Google Translate (a service operated by Google LLC) to obtain a translation. This happens only when you choose this option. The text you translate is processed by Google according to its privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Optional On-Device Speech Models

For offline speech-to-text and text-to-speech, the App can download open-source models (for example from GitHub and Hugging Face). Only the model files are downloaded; once installed, transcription and voice synthesis run entirely on your device and no audio or text is sent to those services. Downloading a model involves a standard request to the hosting provider, which may process technical information such as your IP address as part of delivering the file.

8. Dictionary and Web Search Links

The App can open external dictionary and search websites (for example Cambridge Dictionary, Naver, Google, and other language dictionaries) in your browser to look up a selected word. When you tap such a link, the word and standard browsing information are sent to that third-party website, which is governed by its own privacy policy. The App itself does not send this data to Wonastral.

9. AnkiDroid Integration

If you have the AnkiDroid app installed and choose to create a flashcard, the App adds the card (text and optional media) to your AnkiDroid collection on your device, through AnkiDroid's own interface. This is a local, device-to-device action; no card data is sent to Wonastral. Your AnkiDroid data is handled by AnkiDroid according to its own terms.

10. Background Playback and Notifications

To allow playback to continue while the App is in the background, the App may run a foreground service and show a playback notification with media controls. On Android 13 and later, the App may ask for permission to post notifications. These features operate locally on your device and do not collect personal data.

11. Your Choices

You control all optional online features: they are used only when you enable them or provide the required key. You can remove your Groq API key at any time in Settings, and you can use the App fully offline with on-device models.

You can also reset all app data from the settings ("Reset app data") — which clears settings, API keys, favorites, bookmarks, saved words, generated subtitles, translations, text-to-speech audio, subtitle edits, and downloaded models — or uninstall the App at any time to remove all locally stored data.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided information through one of the optional third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

13. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or store personal data on our side, there is no Wonastral-side database that could be breached. Your files, settings, and API keys remain in the App's private storage on your device. Any data processed by the optional third-party services you choose to use (Groq, Google, model hosts, dictionary sites) is protected by those providers' own security measures.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or in applicable law. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.
